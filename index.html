TWIST

A falling-block game where the world spins.

  • Clearing lines charges your Twist meter.
  • When it's full, press Q or E to spin the whole board. Everything falls, and new lines form.
  • Lines cleared by a twist score ×2.
  • Every level-up, the world turns on its own.
  • Your stack is the song: each column is a beat, each row a note.

Shalom Salaam

Help Avi and Sami get along.

  • Clear a line and they hug. The peace meter goes up.
  • Leave a gap under a block and they squabble. The meter goes down.
  • Fill the meter to start a Peace Festival: double points while they dance.
  • If the meter hits zero, a shouting match adds a junk row.
  • Every 6 lines a dove arrives. It's one block, perfect for filling a gap.
  • Clear 4 at once to sign a Peace Treaty.
